# Compact CM5 AI Camera Carrier (`hardware/rpi-ai-cam-lc`)

Generated from `rea/spec.yaml` by rea-hardware (Lion Circuits Custom tier fab rules, 6-layer 1.6 mm controlled-impedance stack-up, 55.0 x 40.0 mm board outline at origin, mounting holes), then completed and routed for the Compute Module 5 carrier.

## Programming and Debug Path (`decisions`)
- **eMMC Flashing (`rpiboot` over USB-C J1):**
  1. Press and hold recessed boot button `SW2` (Omron `B3U-1000P`, actuated via the front-shell pinhole `F3`), which pulls CM5 `nRPIBOOT` (`J6` pad 93) to `GND` (using CM5's internal `10 kOhm` pull-up to `+3V3` with no redundant external pull-up, and 3.3 V `PESD3V3S1BLYL` ESD TVS `D8` to `GND`).
  2. Connect a host PC to USB-C receptacle `J1` (`USB4105-GF-A`). `J1` supplies `VBUS_IN` (decoupled by `C1` = 2.2 uF and `C4` = 100 nF = `2.3 uF` nominal `cSink` <= 10 uF and protected by TI `TVS2200DRVR` 22 V flat-clamp unidirectional WSON-6 surge TVS `D4` with $V_{\text{RWM}} = 22\text{ V}$ for 20 V USB-C fault tolerance, $V_{\text{CLAMP}} = 27.2\text{ V}$ typ / $27.7\text{ V}$ max at 24 A and $27.6\text{ V}$ typ / $28.0\text{ V}$ max at 40 A $8/20\,\mu\text{s} \le +28\text{ V}$ abs max, and $V_F = -0.5\text{ V}$ typ) via wide `F.Cu` copper polygons, a dedicated 1 oz (`0.035 mm`) `In3.Cu` `VBUS_IN` plane, and 8 parallel `VBUS_IN` vias into two parallel TI `TPS259470ARPWR` active-current-limiting ideal-diode / OVP eFuses (`U4` primary, `U5` secondary; combined $R_{\text{ON(typ)}} = 14.15\text{ m}\Omega$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$ / $21.0\text{ m}\Omega$ max at $85^\circ\text{C}$, mirror-symmetric 9-via `U4.6`/`U5.6` output arrays, 1 oz `In3.Cu` `PWR_5V_PLANE` `/+5V`, and 5 parallel `+5V` vias with `0.50 mm` `B.Cu` bus bars at `J6` pads 77-87 -> `~2 mOhm` total PCB copper+via resistance and `~48 mV` typ / `~110 mV` max carrier drop at 3.0 A) driving all six CM5 `+5v_(Input)` pins (`J6` pads 77, 79, 81, 83, 85, 87). `U4` soft-starts via `C22` = 4.7 nF (`0.43 V/ms`, `~11.7 ms` ramp) so the CM5's internal PMIC input capacitance and carrier `+5V` bulk capacitance (`C30` = 100 uF, `C31` = 100 uF, `C9` = 2.2 uF, `C10..C12` = 3 x 1.0 uF) are isolated from `J1` at hot-plug while `U4`'s open-drain `AUXOFF` holds `U5` `EN/UVLO` low until `U4` achieves full gate overdrive, and `U5`'s open-drain `AUXOFF` (`U5.3`) connects to CM5 `PMIC_Enable` (`J6` pad 99, net `PMIC_ENABLE`) per TI Figure 8-18 to hold the CM5 PMIC in reset until both `U4` and `U5` have completed soft-start and reached full gate overdrive; `C23`/`C24` = 4.7 nF on `U4.10`/`U5.10` (`ITIMER`) set steady-state $t_{\text{ITIMER}} = 3.94\text{ ms}$. `J1` routes `USB2_P`/`USB2_N` directly (through inline ESD array `D1` at `J1`, with `D1` pin 5 on `VBUS_D1_CLAMP` locally decoupled to `GND` by `C25` = 100 nF) to CM5 USB 2.0 OTG (`J7` pads 5/3, CM5 pins 105/103), with `USB_OTG_ID` (`J7` pad 1, CM5 pin 101) left unconnected so the CM5's internal pull-up selects USB Device / `rpiboot` bootloader mode.
  3. The CM5 ROM bootloader enumerates over USB 2.0 OTG for `rpiboot` mass-storage flashing. During reset and `rpiboot`, `22 kOhm` pull-down `R10` holds `USB_VBUS_EN` (`J7` pad 11) at `0 V` so USB-A switches `U2` and `U3` remain disabled and isolated from `J1`.
- **UART0 Debug Console:**
  - Exposed on top-side 1.0 mm SMD test pads `TP1` (`UART0_TXD` / `GPIO14`, `J6` pad 55), `TP2` (`UART0_RXD` / `GPIO15`, `J6` pad 51), and `TP3` (`GND`), referenced to `+3V3` (`GPIO_VREF` on `J6` pad 78 strapped to `+3V3`).
